# Contributing

Use this repository for generalized application behavior, presentation and integration. Shared record protocol and Git-store changes belong in the infrastructure repository. Describe the user-visible problem, explain affected record semantics and provide proportionate validation. Keep account data, signing keys and project records outside the source repository.

The project-authored material is offered under the [MIT licence](LICENSE), with [scope and exceptions](LICENSE.md). By intentionally submitting original work for incorporation, you offer it under MIT while retaining your copyright. Identify third-party material and its licence separately, and state your authority when contributing on another rights holder's behalf.

Add your preferred contributor name or organization and a brief contribution description to [CONTRIBUTORS.md](CONTRIBUTORS.md) when your work is incorporated. Preserve the separate Cytoscape attribution and all other third-party notices. AI tools may be acknowledged as tools, not as copyright holders.
